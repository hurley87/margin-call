# Arcade Leverage is six fixed tiers, enabling O(1) mark-to-market at finalization

Leverage is restricted to exactly six values (`1.25x, 1.50x, 2.00x, 3.00x, 5.00x, 10.00x`) rather than the originally drafted `1.10x–10.00x` continuum. The deciding reason is not UI simplicity: with a continuum, the vault cannot cheaply know a round's total winning liability when the crash point is revealed (it would have to iterate every ticket), which forces share pricing to stay stale between finalization and pull-based claims — a window where anyone, including the keeper who finalizes, could redeem or deposit against a publicly known result at other LPs' expense. With fixed tiers, the vault keeps one aggregate reserved payout per tier per round, `finalizeRound` sums the winning tiers in O(6) into `pendingObligations`, and all share pricing uses `grossAssets − pendingObligations − unrecognizedMargin` (the ERC-4626 `totalAssets()`), closing the sandwich the moment the attestation lands. The remaining pre-attestation window — the plaintext is publicly obtainable offchain from the moment reveal is requested — is closed by the reveal-window freeze on share operations defined in the technical design.

Tiers also make per-tier player capacity exact on the LP Desk, shrink the payout test matrix to six values with exact reach probabilities (7920/6600/4950/3300/1980/990 per 10000, each an identical 0.99× expected gross return), and remove the unreachable 1.10x–1.24x band. Do not "upgrade" leverage to a slider without redesigning the finalization marking mechanism.
